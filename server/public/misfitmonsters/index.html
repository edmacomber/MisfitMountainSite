What's Inside

A match-3 with a sense of place

🌋

60 Levels, 4 Worlds

Ember Wastes, Thornveil Forest, storm-battered Tempest Coast, and frostbound Frostmoor — each biome with its own tile mechanics and painterly backdrop.

🐲

10 Misfit Creatures

Each companion has a signature element and a charged ability that fires when the bar fills. Hatch them from eggs you earn along the way.

🌍

3D Globe Leaderboard

Your scores plot at your real-world location on a rotating night-earth globe. Find your pin glowing gold; tap any player to see their stats.

💎

Cosmetic Unlocks

Classic glass, cat's eye, galactic swirls, frost crystal, electric arcs, and hand-illustrated emerald gemstones — earn marble styles as you climb.

Worlds

Four hand-painted biomes

World 1 · Levels 1–15

Ember Wastes

Volcanic plains under a dark caldera sky. Lava tiles cool when you match atop them.

World 2 · Levels 16–30

Thornveil Forest

Vines, ice, and distant thunder. Spreading vines block swaps until you cut them back.

World 3 · Levels 31–45

Tempest Coast

Storm-lit cliffs above a churning sea. Storm tiles chain-detonate in a lightning cross when matched.

World 4 · Levels 46–60

Frostmoor

Windswept glacial moor under a pale aurora sky. Ice Links tether pairs of tiles — shatter both in the same cascade or neither clears.
